What it does

  • Blocks trackers and ads system-wide. Known tracker and ad domains are blocked using your hosts file — in every app, not just the browser.
  • Cleans junk — and never deletes your logins. Temp and cache cleaning with Identity Keep standing guard, so a clean never signs you out of your bank, email, or streaming. A Windows restore point is created before every clean.
  • Finds duplicates by content, not by name. Files are matched by their actual contents (SHA-256), and you see everything before anything is touched. Paid tiers can remove duplicates — with undo and an audit log.
  • Watches your Downloads. Files that trip plain, published rules — double extensions like invoice.pdf.exe, small unsigned executables, known-bad patterns — are encrypted into quarantine, with one-click restore when a file turns out to be innocent.
  • Ransomware tripwire. Canary files and sudden-change alarms (mass renames, extension surges, folder collapse) that warn you early. It is a tripwire, honestly named — see “What Barbwire Guard is not” below.
  • Repair, in plain language. Runs Windows’ own repair tools (SFC, DISM, CHKDSK) and explains what they found — no proprietary repair magic, just Windows’ tools with a translator. Pro
  • Update Center. App updates you approve one by one. Driver updates are matched to your hardware by hardware ID, come only from the Windows Update catalog and official vendor packages, are installed one at a time — restore point first, device health check after each — and unsigned or non-WHQL drivers are refused outright. Apps: Pro · Drivers: Total
  • No tracking cookies. Third-party and tracking cookies are blocked and expired early — while Identity Keep protects the cookies that keep you signed in. (Any tool promising “no cookies at all” would sign you out of everything; we don’t promise that.)
  • Safe browsing. Malicious and phishing addresses are blocked by the same feed engine that carries the ad, tracker, and cookie rules — four feeds, one engine. Feeds update automatically in Pro
  • Breach check that keeps your address home. Looks up your email against known breaches using Have I Been Pwned’s k-anonymity API — your full address never leaves your machine. Pro
  • Secure shredder. For the files you want gone for good. Pro
  • Bring-your-own-server VPN wizard. Sets up WireGuard on a server you control, kill switch included. We never see your traffic, because it never touches us — there is no Barbwire server. Total

What Barbwire Guard is not

  • Not an antivirus, and not a Defender replacement. Its scanner is a plain-rules tripwire, not a detection engine. Keep Windows Defender on — Barbwire Guard is the second set of eyes next to it.
  • Not a VPN service. No server fleet, no subscriptions, no traffic through us — a setup wizard for your own server.
  • Not a private browser. The launcher opens your installed Chrome or Edge in a hardened, separate profile. The browser engine is Google’s or Microsoft’s.
  • It does not “prevent” ransomware. It detects the early signs and warns you. Any product that promises prevention is selling you a story.
